# VERMONT AVENUE RECORDS
## John's Checklist

Only you can do these. They need your name, phone, photos, or logins.

Torreé handles the website, admin, domain settings, and all tech.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile (do this first)

1. Go to **business.google.com** on a laptop.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Click **Add your business** and type **Vermont Avenue Records**.
4. Fill in:
   - Category: **Rehearsal studio** or **Live music venue**
   - Address: the rehearsal space
   - Phone: your business number
   - Website: https://vermontavenuerecords.com
   - Hours: when people can reach you
5. Add 5 to 10 real photos of the space.
6. Verify it. Choose **video verification** if offered, otherwise watch for a postcard with a code.

Your listing is not public until it is verified.

---

## 2. Get Google Reviews

1. In your profile, click **Ask for reviews**. Copy the link.
2. Save it in your phone as "VA Reviews".
3. Text it the same day after a good session:

> Hey, it's John from Vermont Avenue Records. Thanks for coming through. If you had a good time, mind leaving a quick Google review? It helps us get found. [link]

Ask every band, artist, and jam attendee.

---

## 3. Claim Apple and Bing

- **Apple Maps:** businessconnect.apple.com
- **Bing Places:** bingplaces.com (click "import from Google")

Use the exact same name, address, and phone on both.

---

## 4. Your Domain

Your site is **vermontavenuerecords.com**.

1. Go to **whois.com** and look it up.
2. Log in to the company you bought it from.
3. Turn on **auto renew**.
4. Save the login.

If you do not have the login, tell Torreé.

---

## 5. One Contact Email

Set up **john@vermontavenuerecords.com** or similar.

Put it on Google, Instagram, Facebook, Bandcamp, YouTube, and the website.

---

## 6. Match Your Socials

Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Bandcamp, Spotify.

Same logo, same description, same website link, same email.

Use this line everywhere:
> Vermont Avenue Records is an independent record label and live music collective in Los Angeles. Created by musicians, for musicians.

---

## 7. Ten Minutes Every Monday

1. Reply to Google reviews.
2. Check email.
3. Check Instagram messages.
4. Post one thing.
5. Ask one person for a review.

---

## If That Is Too Much

Do only these four:

1. Create and verify the Google Business Profile.
2. Get ten Google reviews.
3. Confirm the domain is on auto renew.
4. Set up one contact email.

That puts you ahead of most small labels.
